Privacy Policy – Facebook Ads App

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Facebook Ads App (the "App"), developed by Ian Santos, collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you connect your Facebook / Meta account. The App uses the Meta (Facebook) Platform and Marketing API to help you view and manage your advertising accounts, campaigns, and performance data.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in with Facebook and grant permissions, the App may access the following information, limited to the permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information: Your Facebook user ID, name, and email address (if granted).
  • Access tokens: The token issued by Meta that allows the App to act on your behalf.
  • Ad account data: Ad account IDs, names, currency, status, and spending limits.
  • Campaign data: Campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, budgets, schedules, and targeting settings.
  • Performance insights: Metrics such as impressions, reach, clicks, spend, conversions, and related reporting data.
  • Pages and Business assets: Facebook Pages and Business Manager assets you choose to connect, where required to create or manage ads.

We do not access your private messages, friends list, or personal posts, and we do not collect any information beyond what is required for the App to function.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and display your connected ad accounts.
  • To create, edit, pause, or review campaigns, ad sets, and ads at your direction.
  • To display reports, analytics, and performance insights about your ads.
  • To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

Data obtained from Meta is used only to provide the App's features to you, in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not share Meta Platform data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes. Data may only be disclosed:

  • To service providers that host or process data on our behalf (listed below), under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • With your explicit consent.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Facebook Login and the Marketing API. Your use of Facebook is also governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: For secure database storage.
  • Self-Hosted Infrastructure: Certain data is processed on private servers (TrueNAS/Docker) managed by the developer.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Access tokens and account data are stored securely and encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted to the developer for operating the App. We retain your data only as long as your account is connected or as needed to provide the App. Cached advertising and insights data is refreshed from Meta and is not kept longer than necessary.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke access: You can remove the App at any time from your Facebook settings under Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. This immediately stops the App from accessing your data.
  • Access or correction: You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your data at any time (see below).

Depending on where you live (e.g., the EU/EEA, UK, or California), you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. Contact us to exercise them.

7. Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of all data the App has collected from your Facebook account:

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, find the App, and click Remove.
  2. Send an email to ian6502022@gmail.com with the subject "Facebook Ads App – Data Deletion Request", including your name and the email address or Facebook user ID associated with your account.

We will delete your stored profile information, access tokens, and any cached advertising data within 30 days of receiving your request and will confirm by email once completed. Ads and campaigns that exist inside your Meta ad account are owned and managed by Meta and are not deleted by this process; you can manage them directly in Meta Ads Manager.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for businesses and adults managing advertising. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: ian6502022@gmail.com
