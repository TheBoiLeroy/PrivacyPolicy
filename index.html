Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected and used across apps developed by Ian Santos (including aCheck, ContractorWorks, and the Facebook Ads App).

1. General Data Handling

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is stored securely and used only to provide core app functionality.

2. App-Specific Disclosures

aCheck

Data Collected: Name, email, and check-in activity.

Usage: This data is stored in Supabase to allow you to track your check-in history across devices.

Health Sync (optional): If enabled, aCheck reads workout data from Apple Health or Health Connect on your device to simplify check-ins. Workout details are not uploaded to our servers.

See the full aCheck Privacy Policy, including health data use and deletion.

ContractorWorks (Contractor App)

Precise Location Data: This app collects precise GPS location data only during the "Clock In" and "Clock Out" process to verify job site attendance. This data is not tracked in the background.

Camera and Photos: The app requires access to your camera to capture "Before" and "After" job photos as required by your employer/manager. As well as Invoice building if using integrated feature

Organization Data: Information such as work descriptions and hours worked are shared only with the "Boss" account associated with your Organization ID.

Facebook Ads App

Data Collected: Facebook profile basics (user ID, name, email), access tokens, and ad account, campaign, and performance insights data that you authorize via Facebook Login.

Usage: Used only to display and manage your ads. Never sold or shared for marketing.

See the full Facebook Ads App Privacy Policy and Data Deletion Instructions.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to ensure app stability and functionality:

  • Supabase: For secure database storage.
  • OneSignal: For push notifications (aCheck).
  • Google Maps API: For address geocoding and location verification.
  • Self-Hosted Infrastructure: Certain data is processed on private servers (TrueNAS/Docker) managed by the developer.
